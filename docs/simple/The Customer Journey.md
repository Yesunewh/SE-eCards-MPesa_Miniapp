# The Customer Journey

Back to [[00 Home]] · Technical version: [[Flow Walkthrough]]

Meet **Dawit**. He wants a $50 Netflix gift card.

### 1. He opens eCards inside M-Pesa
He taps the eCards tile in his M-Pesa app. M-Pesa asks once: *"Allow eCards to see your name and phone number?"* He taps **Allow**. Now eCards knows who he is. There is no sign-up form and no password.

### 2. He browses
The home screen shows banners, popular brands and categories (gaming, video, music, apps…). He taps **Netflix** and sees the values: $10, $25, $50… each with its **price in Birr**.

### 3. He adds it to the cart and checks out
He chooses $50, then **Buy**. He can choose to receive the code by SMS (his number is filled in) or by email.

### 4. He pays with M-Pesa
The normal M-Pesa payment screen appears, showing the amount, the business name and the order number (like `EC12345678`). He enters his **PIN**.

### 5. He gets his code, instantly
A few seconds later the app shows his **Netflix code** in "My Orders". He can copy it and tap "I used this code" to keep his wallet tidy. (Once SMS is connected, he also gets it by text.)

### What if…
- **…he cancels the payment?** Nothing is charged. He can tap Pay again.
- **…the card is out of stock?** He can still buy it as a **pre-order**. The app shows the expected date, and the code appears automatically when it arrives (usually within days).
- **…he has a problem?** He opens **Support** in the app and writes a message. Staff reply in the same chat.
- **…he opens eCards on a different phone?** He sees his orders, but codes are shown only on the phone that bought them. This protects him if someone pretends to be him.

### Languages
English, Amharic, Afaan Oromo, Somali and Tigrinya.
