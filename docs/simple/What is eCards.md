# What is eCards? (the 5-minute version)

Back to [[00 Home]]

## The idea in one sentence
**eCards lets anyone in Ethiopia buy international gift cards (Netflix, PlayStation, Steam, Apple, Google Play and more) with M-Pesa, right inside the M-Pesa app, and get the card instantly.**

## The problem it solves
Many online services need a foreign card (Visa or Mastercard) to pay. Most people in Ethiopia don't have one. A **gift card** is a prepaid code that works instead: you type it into Netflix, PlayStation and so on, and the value is added to your account.

Buying gift cards today often means informal sellers, Telegram groups, waiting and trust issues. eCards makes it:
- **official**: inside the M-Pesa app people already trust;
- **instant**: the code appears seconds after paying;
- **safe**: paid through M-Pesa, with a receipt and support.

## Who uses it
- **Customers.** M-Pesa users who want to pay for games, streaming, apps or mobile top-ups abroad.
- **Our staff.** They use the admin panel to manage prices, stock, orders and customer questions.

## How it makes money
We buy gift cards wholesale (from our supplier **Wupex**, or other suppliers) in US dollars. We sell them in Birr at a price set by an exchange rate that includes our margin. VAT (15 %) is included in the price.

## How it works, in 4 steps
1. **Open** eCards in the M-Pesa app. It recognises you, so there is no new account or password.
2. **Choose** a card and value, for example Netflix $50.
3. **Pay** with M-Pesa: the usual M-Pesa screen and your PIN.
4. **Get** your code on screen right away (and by SMS once SMS sending is connected).

If a card is temporarily out of stock, you can still buy it as a **pre-order**. You see the expected date, and the code arrives automatically when stock comes in.

## Where we are
The product is **built and working in a test setup**. We are waiting for Safaricom (mini app portal access and the business number) and Wupex (production keys; their test account already works) to connect real money and real cards. See [[What Is Done and What Is Next]].
