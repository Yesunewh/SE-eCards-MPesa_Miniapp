# Demo Guide – Simple (talk track)

Back to [[00 Home]] · Setup steps: [[Demo Guide – Technical#0. Prepare (5 minutes before)]]

Use this when the audience is business, management or partners. Do the same clicks as the technical demo, but **say this**.

---

### Opening (30 seconds)
> "Most people here can't pay Netflix, PlayStation or Apple directly because they need a foreign card. eCards fixes that: buy the gift card with M-Pesa, inside the M-Pesa app, and get it in seconds. Let me show you."

### 1. The shop
*(Show the home screen.)*
> "This is what the customer sees inside M-Pesa. All the popular brands, prices already in Birr. We control everything you see here, the banners, the order and the prices, from our back office."

### 2. No sign-up
*(Sign in.)*
> "Inside M-Pesa there's no registration. M-Pesa asks the customer once to share their name and number with us, and they're in."

### 3. Buying
*(Choose Netflix $50 → Buy → Pay.)*
> "They pick a card and pay with the normal M-Pesa screen and PIN. Today this is a test payment. No real money moves in this demo."

*(The code appears.)*
> "And here's the code, instantly. They copy it into Netflix and they're done. It also stays in their wallet in the app, and once SMS is connected, they'll get it by text too."

### 4. Our back office
*(Switch to the admin window and open the same order.)*
> "On our side we see the order immediately. Notice we only see the last four characters of the code. Staff can reveal it to help a customer, but every reveal is recorded."

### 5. Out of stock? Still a sale.
*(Buy the out-of-stock card.)*
> "If we run out, we don't lose the sale. The customer pays and gets a pre-order with a date."

*(Add a code in Stock.)*
> "When new stock arrives, the system delivers it automatically to whoever was waiting first. Watch the customer's phone…" *(The code appears.)* "…there it is."

### 6. Support
*(Open a ticket in the app, then reply from admin.)*
> "Questions come straight into our support inbox, linked to the order, and we reply in the app."

### 7. Where we are
> "Everything you saw is built and tested: more than a hundred automated checks run on every change. It's running on our own server.
> To go live we need three things: **Safaricom's mini app portal access**, **confirmation of our business number**, and **Wupex's API keys** so we can buy codes automatically. After that, it's testing inside the real M-Pesa app, and then launch."

### Closing
> "Simple for customers, safe for the money, and very little manual work for us."

## Questions people usually ask
| Question | Short answer |
|---|---|
| Is the money safe? | We only treat an order as paid when M-Pesa itself confirms it, and the amount must match. |
| What if two people buy the last card at once? | Only one gets it; the other becomes a pre-order. A code is never sold twice. |
| What if Wupex is down? | We sell from our own stock, or take a pre-order and deliver later. |
| Can staff steal codes? | Codes are encrypted, staff see only 4 characters, and every reveal is logged. |
| Which languages? | English, Amharic, Afaan Oromo, Somali, Tigrinya. |
| How fast is delivery? | A few seconds after paying, when in stock or available from Wupex. |
| How do we make money? | We buy in USD at wholesale and sell in Birr at a rate that includes our margin. |
