# Help

URL: https://ecards.example.com/help/

## Questions and answers

### How fast do I get my code?

Cards in stock are delivered seconds after you approve the M-Pesa payment. The code shows on screen, in My orders, and by SMS or email.

### How do I pay?

With M-Pesa. Enter your M-Pesa number at checkout and approve the payment prompt on your phone with your PIN. We never ask for your PIN on this website.

### What is a pre-order?

When a card is out of stock you can still buy it. You pay now and we send the code automatically when the card arrives. The expected date is shown before you pay.

### Can I send a gift card to someone else?

Yes. At checkout choose SMS or email and enter their phone number or email address. They receive the code directly.

### Can I get a refund?

No. Gift card codes are digital and can’t be returned or refunded once delivered. If a code doesn’t work, contact us and we will help.

### Do I need an account?

You sign in with your phone number and a one-time SMS code. That keeps your orders and codes in one place.

## Terms of service

eCards sells digital gift card codes for use with the brands shown. Prices are in Ethiopian Birr and include 15% VAT. An order is confirmed when M-Pesa confirms your payment.

Codes are for personal use and may not be resold. Each brand’s own terms apply when you redeem a code.

## No-refund policy

All sales are final. Gift card codes can’t be returned, exchanged or refunded once delivered, because a delivered code can be used straight away.

If a code doesn’t work, or a pre-order is late, contact us with your order number and we will make it right.

## Privacy policy

We keep your name, phone number, the email or number you send codes to, and your orders. We use them to deliver your codes and help you. We don’t sell your data.

Payments are handled by M-Pesa; we never see or store your M-Pesa PIN.
