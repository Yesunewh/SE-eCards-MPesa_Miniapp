## Key facts

- Prices are in Ethiopian Birr (ETB) and include 15% VAT. 1 US dollar of card value = Br 265.
- Payment: M-Pesa only. The customer approves the payment on their phone with their PIN.
- Delivery: the code shows on screen and in My orders, and is sent by SMS or email, usually within seconds. Out-of-stock cards can be pre-ordered.
- All sales are final: gift card codes can’t be refunded once delivered.
